♿  Electric wheelchairs with a lithium battery (Carry-on :blue[O], checked baggage :red[X])
- Maximum capacity for removable lithium batteries 300Wh or less (carried by passengers)
- Spare batteries (other than wheelchair batteries)
    - 1 battery: 300Wh or less
    - 2 batteries: 160Wh or less each
- If a battery is not removable, you must make sure it is attached securely on the electric wheelchair and take the necessary measures to prevent short circuits.
    - You cannot carry the batteries if short circuits cannot be prevented.

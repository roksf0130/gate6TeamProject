🔌 How to calculate Wh when the voltage is labeled but the amount of energy (Wh) is not labeled
|Labeled|Formula|Capacity|
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
|15,000mAh, 3.7V|(15,000 ÷ 1,000) X 3.7|55Wh|
|15Ah, 3.7V|15 X 3.7|55Wh|
- Electric charge (Ah) X voltage (V) = amount of energy (Wh)
- 1Ah = 1,000mAh
- Products without a voltage (V) labeled may not be allowed to be transported as the capacity is unknown.
